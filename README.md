# K-pop Consumption: DearData Project
IN 1007 Creative Coding Dear data project.

**About the project**

K-pop is something I regularly engage with through different platforms, whether it's listening to music, watching Youtube videos, scrolling through Tiktok or engaging with artists post on Instagram.
For this project, I am collecting data on my own K-pop consumption over the course of one week. I want to understand how much k-pop content I engage with, which platforms I use most and what types of content I spend the most of my time on.
To do this, I am recording my activity in real time to see whether there are patterns I might not have noticed I was doing.

**Research Question**

_How do my K-pop consumption habits vary across different platforms, content types and times of day over the course of two weeks?_

This question will help me with my data collection and decide how to represent my findings through a visualiser.

**Data Collection**

Each observation represents an instance of K-pop content I've engaged with during my two week timeframe. 

I am recording the following:

* Date: When I engaged with the content
* Time of day: When the activity took place
* Platform: Where I accessed the content, e.g., Youtube, Tiktok, Spotify, Instagram, etc
* Content type: What I engaged with, e.g., music videos, performances dance challenges or social media posts
* Artist: The group or soloists I engaged with
* Artist category: Whether the artist is part of a boy group, girl group, soloist, etc
* Duration: How long was the individual content 
* Time spent: How long did I spend on that content
* Repeated?: Did I repeat it or did I only watch/engage with it once?
* Engagement: Whether I listened, liked, comment, shared or left multiple actions

My aim is to collect at least 100 observations by Part 2 of the Project.

**Part 1 Development**

For part 1, I will develop a processing sketch that uses my collected data so far to create a visual representation of my K-pop consumption habits.

The development will include:
* collecting at least 25 observations
* preparing my dataset so it can be used in the Processing
* developing the beginning of a visualiser that communicated my patterns and habits in my data
* using objects and methods to structure my code
* experimenting with different visual elements, e.g. shaped, colours, sizes, positioning
* documenting my progress and making regular commits to GitHub

The visualisation should allow viewers to explore my k-pop consumption differs from different platforms, contents types and times of day.
